How this works. This page has no server — it edits posts in your browser and hands you an updated posts.json. To publish: fill in a post → Add / update post → Download posts.json → replace assets/posts.json in your repo → commit & push. Your live blog updates on the next deploy. Existing posts load automatically when this page is served over http (locally via serve.py, or live).

Body supports light Markdown: ## Heading, ### Subheading, **bold**, - list items, [link](https://…), and blank lines between paragraphs.
