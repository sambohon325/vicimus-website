Overview

Turn dealership data into confident decisions.

What if every department was working from the same trusted view of performance? Pie brings clarity to complex dealership data, highlighting the metrics that matter, removing noise, and presenting insights that can be understood at a glance. No clutter, no hunting — just clear visibility into the health of your operation.

Key capabilities

Performance, made visible.

01

Unified Reporting

Consolidating 100% of your operational data into a single reporting layer eliminates silos so performance is evaluated in context, not isolation.

02

Smart Metrics

The KPIs that drive outcomes are normalized, benchmarked, and trended over time to surface what's improving, what's declining, and where attention is required.

03

Operational Visibility

From inventory aging and fixed coverage to revenue per VIN and technician efficiency, spot bottlenecks and outliers before they impact profitability.

04

Automated Insights

Pie doesn't just show the numbers — it surfaces the why, tracing a trend back to the departments, advisors, and timeframes driving it.
